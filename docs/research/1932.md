At 18 px it is visible. A fresh second native run produces byte-identical 18/20 px question images. This is a visible S14/S22, Q-A2/Q-A10 and UX30-13 failure even though the Conversations capture command exits 0: that route does not invoke the menu overflow measurement. [Screen 04](../design/evidence/1932/screens/04-answer-send-missing-20.png). Filed [#1991](https://github.com/taskqorg/taskq/issues/1991), follow-up to #1929; read back open with `q-held` and without `taskq`.

**F2 — incomplete-history warning trips the native guard.** Home and Settings, dark/light, 16/20 px all report `button 6 by 3 px`; the capture command exits 1. The notification button's unread text is 6 and its separate warning has `bottom:-3px;right:-3px` in `src/app-shell.css`. The marker is visibly present in the images; no root overflow was reported. This is a failed guard requiring reconciliation, not proof that three pixels of essential label text are missing. [Screen 01](../design/evidence/1932/screens/01-home-16.png). Filed [#1992](https://github.com/taskqorg/taskq/issues/1992); read back open with `q-held` and without `taskq`.

Neither implementation is fixed here. Before failures are preserved; an after-fix pass is unavailable. [Seven representative native artifacts](../design/evidence/1932/checks.md#selected-review-set) include five full webview frames and two Summary rasters. They are candidate captures, not historical prototypes or a completed before/after correction. There is no interaction recording.
