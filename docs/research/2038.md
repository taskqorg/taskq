# Unaccepted spawns on cs#528, attributed from the Mac host

## Question

[#2020](https://github.com/taskqorg/taskq/issues/2020) left the cause of the cs#528 spawns without an accepted entry partly unknown: about 22 of them looked later than the [#1903](https://github.com/taskqorg/taskq/issues/1903) readback loop. Which cause posted each unaccepted spawn? Only the Mac host can read the evidence: it holds the GitLab token for cs and runs the only service that serves it.

## Method

- Notes: all notes of [cs#528](https://gitlab.ufobe.com/gradus/cyprusbz/-/issues/528), read on 2026-10-31 from the Mac host through the GitLab API (`GET projects/:id/issues/528/notes`, 3 pages, 205 notes, 187 of them non-system).
- Classifier: `2020-spawns.py` from the #2020 branch ([commit 432f4e49](https://github.com/taskqorg/taskq/blob/432f4e49a9b562df88cf2be34133e974edb44de6/docs/research/2020-spawns.py)), run unchanged on the saved notes as `{"gitlab": [...]}`.
- Service log: the Mac service log of the cs project, which starts on 2026-10-26, so it covers every spawn on cs#528. Matched lines: `#528 spawn <id> claim unknown`, `lost the claim`, `released`, `gone`, `update:` and the service start banner.
- Times are UTC. Session ids from the log are left out.

## Findings

### A1. Classes

Classifier output: `spawn 92 accepted-entries 23 classes {'accepted': 19, 'no-start': 1, 'lost': 72} first-accepted 2026-10-29T13:49:40.662Z`.

- Every spawn on cs#528 is by the `mac` host; no two-host race is possible here.
- All 73 unaccepted spawns fall inside one window of 53 minutes, 2026-10-30 07:24:50Z to 08:17:45Z, about one every 44 s.
- Since 08:17:45Z the issue has 6 spawns, all accepted: 25160 (08:20:07Z), 25167 (08:21:18Z), 25334 (09:04:35Z), 25472 (09:38:27Z), 25550 (10:08:32Z) and 29997 (2026-10-31 06:00:35Z).

### A2. Cause of each unaccepted spawn

The Mac log has one `#528 spawn <id> claim unknown: readback lacks the spawn` line for each of the 73 unaccepted spawns, 2 to 5 s after the note's board time. It has no `lost the claim` line for cs at all, and no other `claim unknown` line for any cs task.

- **24958, no-start.** The first spawn of the window. Fold took it: the task was `ready` and this was the first `spawn`. The service could not see its own note in the readback, so it never started the child. After the restart the new binary wrote `#528 lost spawn 24958: no attempt log on mac 3193 s after the spawn; released no-start` (08:19:16Z), then `released 24958 no-start` and `gone attempt 24958`. Cause: #1903 readback loop, recovered by the restart.
- **The 72 lost spawns.** Fold ignored each one, because the task was already `doing` under 24958. The service again saw no spawn in its readback, logged `claim unknown` and claimed again on the next pass. Cause: #1903 readback loop. No spawn was released or lost the claim for another reason.
- **The loop stopped at a restart.** The log shows the service on `4.6.47-dev.1793341075` (built 06:17:55Z) from 06:19:14Z. The #1903 fix, [PR #1904](https://github.com/taskqorg/taskq/pull/1904), merged at 08:15:08Z. The update to `4.6.47-dev.1793348133` (built 08:15:33Z) logged at 08:17:46Z; the old process posted its last spawn, 25150, at 08:17:45Z; the new service started at 08:17:53Z. No spawn was lost after it. The log does not record the build's source commit, so the presence of the fix in that build is shown by timing and by the stop, not by a commit id.
- **No other restart inside the window.** Between 06:19:14Z and 08:17:46Z the log has no `update:` line other than `up to date` and no service start banner.

### Spawns after 2026-10-30 08:17Z

- **25148** (08:17:08Z) and **25150** (08:17:45Z): lost, both by the old binary, both with a `claim unknown` line. They are the last two spawns of the loop.
- The first spawn by the new binary is 25160 (08:20:07Z), accepted. All 6 spawns since then are accepted.

### The #2020 estimate of 22 later spawns

#2020 derived about 22 unaccepted spawns after the loop window from two counts: 64 spawns at the #1905 filing (08:17Z) and 91 at the #1984 read. The notes show 86 spawns posted by 08:17:45Z (13 accepted, 73 unaccepted), so the 64 was an earlier, partial count. No unaccepted spawn follows the loop; the 22 do not exist.

## Options

1. **Close #2020 A1 on this evidence.** Plus: every unaccepted spawn on cs#528 has a cause from the log. Minus: none found.
2. **Also add the build source commit to the service start line.** Plus: a later attribution could prove which fix a build contains. Minus: a code change for a question this evidence already answers by timing.

## Verdict

Confirmed: all 73 unaccepted spawns on cs#528 come from the #1903 readback loop on the Mac host, 2026-10-30 07:24:50Z to 08:17:45Z. 72 are lost (fold took 24958 first) and one, 24958, was released as no-start after the restart. No unaccepted spawn exists after 08:17:45Z. The candidate causes of #2020 (a fold-rejected claim loop, two-host races, a second loop) do not occur.
