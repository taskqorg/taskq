### Preservation and interpretation

Snapshot children drop inherited attempt/session/board-token bindings and disable incident reporting. The monitor probe reads OS state and restores its own thread context. This explains the unqualified boundary; it is not a measured defect or a reason to remove support. A renderer export, taskbar button icon and actual notification-area slot are distinct observations (I6/R12).

No product, contract, assertion or permission changed (R13). This is evidence work; there is no product fix for which a failure-before/pass-after result can be claimed.
